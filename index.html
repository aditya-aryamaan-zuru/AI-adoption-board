    About the data

    Who's in the cohort
    The 45 NZ summer interns who started on 16 November 2026, from ZURU's people data.
    What counts as using a skill
    A SkillHub skill activated in Claude or WorkBuddy. Built-ins such as docx or xlsx aren't counted, and you only see skills you're allowed to see.
    How fresh it is
    Live: the page reads SkillHub as you each time it loads, so a skill used a minute ago shows up on refresh. Days are UTC.
    "Not seen yet" isn't "didn't do it"
    Work emails are still being set up for most interns, so activity is matched on the usual firstname.lastname@zuru.com address. If yours is different, it may not show yet. Tell the AI Integration team.
    Privacy
    The page shows cohort totals only. Your own steps are worked out in your browser from your own sign-in; nobody else's names are loaded.
    Coming next
    Tiers on the official AI ladder (T0–T7) once the cohort appears in the tier data, which runs a few days behind.
